# Dalbit listing workflow

Identify the product before drafting, logging keywords, or changing Etsy. Use a user-supplied SKU when available. Otherwise, read the live Product Master and resolve the SKU from the user's product name, listing URL or ID, photo, folder, or other identifying context. Match a listing ID or URL directly to a Master row when that association is recorded there; otherwise compare product details and photos. Require one unique Master row supported by the available evidence; do not guess from a similar product, a partial SKU, or an Etsy title alone. If several rows remain plausible, ask the user to choose among the candidates. An Etsy listing URL or ID identifies an existing listing when provided; screenshots and the local `C:\Users\sshoo\Dalbit\<SKU>` photos can establish visible product details. Read the keyword database for copy and tag work, including before an intake question for that work; photo-only requests do not need it.

## Product and keyword workflow

Steps 3-6 apply to copy and tag work. For a photo-only request, complete steps 1-2, then use step 7.

1. Read the current Product Master and brand guide. Match the user-supplied SKU, or find the SKU in the Master by comparing the user's identifying context with current rows and, when useful, local SKU photos. Confirm exactly one row and record its full SKU, including any variant suffix. If no row or multiple rows match, ask for clarification before drafting or changing Etsy. Inspect supplied screenshots or local SKU photos for visible product type, motifs, color, and hardware; do not identify gemstone composition, plating, or dimensions from appearance alone.
2. Use the Etsy API for the current listing whenever it is available. If the user supplied a listing URL or ID, extract its numeric listing ID and call `etsy_get_listing`; compare that listing with the candidate Master row and product photos before accepting the SKU match. Otherwise, page through `etsy_list_listings` and compare candidates with the exact Product Master row and available product photos. Use a candidate only when the product identity is unambiguous; if it is not, ask for the listing URL or ID. Confirm the listing belongs to `DalbitStudioStore` and that its product details match the SKU. The API's title and description may be outdated marketing copy, not proof of materials, size, or cultural meaning. Treat API `tags` and `materials` as separate fields, even if their contents look misplaced. If the API is unavailable, use the supplied link or screenshots and disclose the gap. Keep unverified facts out of customer copy and flag them separately.
3. Read the `Global keyword` tab, the live `Keywords` library, and relevant `Usage` and `Listings` records. Also read the current `Keyword market` rows for candidate phrases that accurately fit the product. Use global terms as broad prompts and market metrics as evidence for prioritizing relevant wording; do not force broad or misleading terms into copy. Preserve each metric's source, period, definition, and availability. Treat Etsy searches as demand estimates for the stated period, Listadum listing counts/difficulty as competition signals, and top-100 views/favorites or modeled revenue as imperfect context rather than proof of monthly demand or sales. Do not infer search volume or conversion when a metric is missing or the lookup limit was reached. Choose a clear primary product phrase and complementary terms for verified materials, motifs, fit and uses. Inspect source evidence and reuse guidance. A phrase being used frequently in this shop does not establish demand, ranking or sales performance. Longer phrases can inform a title or description without becoming a tag verbatim. Do not copy an entire unrelated product's tag set or force differentiation where the same term is relevant to both products.
4. Draft one readable title of at most 140 characters, a warm description, and exactly 13 distinct relevant tags of at most 20 characters each, including spaces. Lead the title with the product and distinguishing features. Never put size, length, or other measurements in the title; place verified measurements in Details. Use keywords naturally in the opening description. Cover different relevant search intents without repetitive keyword lists. If facts are too sparse for 13 truthful tags, deliver the supported copy and state the specific gap.
5. Follow the brand guide's description structure: warm product introduction, a wearing or gifting sentence, a short details list of verified facts, and signature bojagi wrapping copy unless an exception applies. Keep the voice gentle, clear and restrained. Avoid generic “Korean inspired” wording; name supported cultural motifs or describe the design without inventing cultural origin or symbolism. Do not invent handmade status, materials, health claims or guaranteed good luck. Keep missing or unverified facts out of customer-facing copy and flag them separately.
6. For copy work, validate title and tag lengths, duplicate tags, product relevance and source conflicts. Log newly drafted title phrases and tags in the keyword database using the linked procedure. Record exact current Etsy title and tags as Observed only when verified through `etsy_get_listing`; do not call new draft terms Observed. This skill authorizes keyword database updates. A request to draft or review does not authorize an Etsy edit. When the user explicitly asks to apply the copy to the identified existing listing, assemble the DESCRIPTION, DETAILS, and OUR SIGNATURE WRAPPING sections into one customer-facing Etsy description. Call `etsy_get_listing` immediately before `etsy_update_listing`, pass its `updated_timestamp` as `expected_updated_timestamp`, and update only the requested title, description, tags, or materials. Preserve other listing fields. If the timestamp changed, re-read and reconcile before retrying; do not overwrite an intervening edit. Read back the listing to verify the result. Do not change the Product Master, Listadum, or unrelated Drive files. For approved new drafts, use the API creation procedure below; publishing requires explicit authorization.
7. When the user asks to add photos or complete the media sequence, first verify the exact SKU and Etsy listing as above. Inspect the local SKU folder for photos and video and any matching symbol card in `C:\Users\sshoo\Dalbit\Stone Cards`, and use the photo template below. Select and prepare the rank-1 hero using the thumbnail-editing instructions below; other photo slots use their original, unzoomed assets. Call `etsy_get_listing_images` and `etsy_get_listing_videos` to see the current media and avoid duplicates. Follow the video fallback below; when uploading a local MP4 or MOV to a listing with no active video, pass the current video IDs to `etsy_upload_listing_video` and verify the returned video ID. Select only photos that accurately show this product; use a meaning card only when its element is confirmed for this SKU. Upload JPEG or PNG files with `etsy_upload_listing_image`, passing the ordered `expected_image_ids` from the fresh image read. The tool appends by default and never overwrites; specify a rank when needed to place the photo in the template order. For multiple photos, read the images again after each upload before the next one. Add concise factual alt text when useful. Verify each uploaded image ID and position. Do not replace or remove existing media unless the user explicitly asks. A copy-only request does not authorize media uploads.
8. For completed copy work, use the following output outline. Preserve the exact section names and order. The user's example establishes presentation, not product facts: never carry its materials, measurements, motif, symbolism, or card choice into another SKU without verification. Keep the title, description, details, wrapping and tags as copy-ready listing content. Write a short opening paragraph, a fuller product paragraph, and a cultural context paragraph only when its claims are verified for the exact product. The Details list contains only verified facts; omit a Materials line if material is unknown rather than putting an internal placeholder in listing copy. After Tags, place photo-card guidance in a standalone sentence without a heading, as in the user's example. Name one recommended lead card and briefly explain its product match. Mention a supporting card only if the product master or user-provided product facts confirm the relevant feature. If a card was uploaded, state its resulting position instead of presenting it as a recommendation. If Drive assets are unavailable or the match is uncertain, say so instead of guessing. Use these second-level headings in this exact order:

## TITLE

[One title, at most 140 characters]

## DESCRIPTION

[Warm opening and product details in short paragraphs]

## DETAILS

- [Verified fact]
- [Verified fact]

## OUR SIGNATURE WRAPPING

[Use the brand guide's bojagi paragraph, adapting “piece” to the product]

## TAGS

[Exactly 13 distinct relevant tags, comma separated; each no more than 20 characters]

[Standalone photo-card recommendation sentence, separate from the Etsy listing copy; optional verified supporting card]

If needed, follow with **NEEDS CONFIRMATION** for unresolved facts, then a concise **Sources checked** line naming sources actually read, access gaps, whether the keyword log was updated, and whether an Etsy edit was applied and verified. For media-only work, report the hero source and edited thumbnail filename, video source or skipped slot, uploaded filenames, Etsy media IDs and positions, and any verification gap; the copy outline is unnecessary. Unavailable sources should not prevent a useful supported draft; disclose the specific limitation and leave a retryable unsaved log when a database write fails.

## Creating approved new drafts through the API

The registered MCP tools do not expose draft creation. For approved new listings, use the existing authenticated `etsyRequest` helper in the workspace's `integrations/etsy-listings/etsy.mjs` with Etsy's `createDraftListing` endpoint. The local batch helper supplies a working request shape; verify current shop and fulfillment profile IDs and check for duplicates before creating. Use the approved product facts, price, quantity, weight and dimensions, set the exact SKU through inventory, and verify the saved draft and media. Keep it unpublished unless publishing is explicitly authorized. Do not run `publish-batch.mjs` unchanged for draft-only work: it activates listings. Prefer this API route over browser entry.

## Hero thumbnail preparation

Before preparing or revising a rank-1 hero, read [references/hero-styling.md](references/hero-styling.md). Preserve the original SKU photo, its background, lighting and actual jewelry. Borrow only the example's elegant title typography and short sentiment treatment, using Product Master-derived wording; add thin dividers above and below the title that echo the approved Dalbit cards' design language. Plan the overlay and final framing together, aiming for the complete bracelet to occupy about **40% of the final frame** where the original allows. This is product fill, not a 40% enlargement. Do not generate a new scene, replace or extend the background, add floral props, or redraw the product. Keep 1:1 for the current batch. The reference owns detailed layout, wording, preservation and save rules. This treatment applies only to the rank-1 hero.

**Thumbnail approval checkpoint:** Show the actual zoomed-in thumbnail inline, label it with its SKU, and provide its saved path before creating or updating the Etsy listing, changing listing copy, or uploading media. For a batch, show every proposed hero and make clear which SKUs the approval covers. Ask the user to approve the preview or request changes. Approval of an earlier workflow stage or general permission to work on listings does not approve a thumbnail the user has not seen. Prepare copy and the media plan locally while awaiting feedback. Once the user approves the exact preview and the proposed Etsy changes, use that approved file for rank 1 within the connected API's supported capabilities. If the thumbnail changes after approval, show the revised image and obtain approval again before applying it.

## Etsy photo template

### Five-second product video

When preparing a local product video for upload, review the footage and choose the best continuous five-second segment yourself. Prefer the least camera shake, natural movement, clear focus, useful light, and an unobstructed view that shows the bracelet, its focal pieces, and its overall shape effectively. Compare candidate segments rather than automatically taking the opening five seconds. Trim the chosen segment to five seconds; preserve the natural playback speed, colors, and product appearance, and avoid abrupt cuts, transitions, or added effects. Preserve the original and save a separate `<SKU>_etsy_video_5s.mp4` in the matching SKU folder. Inspect the finished clip from start to end and include the clip and its source time range in the media review packet before upload. If the source is shorter than five seconds or has no usable stable segment, report that limitation rather than padding, looping, or inventing footage. This rule prepares a local video when upload is needed; the existing rule for preserving an active Etsy video still applies unless the user requests replacement.

Use this order for the full Etsy media presentation. `01 Video` is separate from the numbered listing images: image rank 1 is the hero, rank 2 is gift presentation, rank 3 is size and materials, then come detail photos, worn or use photos, and meaning cards last. For slot 01, use a matching video from the SKU folder when available and the listing has no active video. If there is no local video, keep an active video already on the Etsy listing. If neither source has a video, skip slot 01 and continue with gift presentation; do not block the photo sequence. When both a local video and an active listing video exist, preserve the listed one unless the user specifically asks to replace it. If a source image for another slot is missing, report the gap rather than filling it with an unrelated asset.

| Sequence | Media | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 00 | Main product hero | Original product photo with a Product Master-derived title and sentiment, card-style top/bottom dividers, and about 40% product fill where original framing allows. |
| 01 | Video | Product video, displayed separately from photo ranks. |
| 02 | Gift presentation | Packaging and unboxing experience. |
| 03 | Size and materials | Information image showing size, closure, and verified materials. |
| 04 | Details | Close-up photos, such as charm, bead, and closure details; use more than one when helpful. |
