# Dalbit Studio Cultural Symbol Card Design Guide

**Status:** Living design system  
**Current version:** 1.2  
**Last updated:** 2026-09-21

## Purpose

This guide defines the reusable visual and content system for Dalbit Studio's Korean cultural symbol cards. Each card explains one featured bracelet element through a symbolic illustration, its Korean or primary name, an English subtitle, and three concise meanings.

The system should feel recognizably Dalbit across the full collection without forcing every element into the same color palette. The layout remains consistent; the palette and central imagery respond to the featured element.

## Core principles

1. **Korean cultural grounding** — Use motifs, framing, and symbolism that are relevant to Korean heritage. Avoid generic “Asian” decoration or mixing unrelated cultural motifs.
2. **Symbol first** — The center shows a clean vector-style emblem of the featured element, never a bracelet photograph.
3. **Soft, modern tradition** — Combine traditional Korean references with a gentle, polished, contemporary presentation.
4. **Element-led color** — Choose colors from the featured element and its cultural atmosphere. The only universal palette constraint is soft, muted pastel color.
5. **Clear educational hierarchy** — A viewer should immediately understand the element's name and three meanings.
6. **Cultural care** — Treat meaning statements as concise editorial interpretations. Verify unfamiliar or contested symbolism before publishing it as fact.

## Fixed card structure

- Square 1:1 canvas; current production cards are 1254 × 1254 px.
- Light hanji-like paper texture across the background.
- Fine geometric corner borders inspired by Korean lattice or restrained dancheong geometry.
- Soft crescent moon and cloud header.
- Small centered `Dalbit Studio` wordmark below the header.
- Large uppercase primary title.
- Smaller uppercase English subtitle directly beneath the title.
- Large centered standalone vector emblem placed directly on the hanji background.
- Vertical `DALBIT STUDIO` branding on both sides.
- Wide rounded footer panel containing three evenly spaced icons and meanings.
- Balanced symmetry, generous breathing room, and readable contrast.

## Variable card content

### Title

- Prefer the Korean name or established romanization when it helps express the cultural identity.
- Use uppercase display lettering.
- Verify spelling before final generation.
- Examples: `MAEHWA`, `YEONGEUN`, `BOKJUMEONI`.

### Subtitle

- Place the plain-English identification directly below the title.
- Use uppercase lettering with wider tracking and a smaller scale.
- Examples: `PLUM BLOSSOM`, `LOTUS ROOT`, `LUCKY POUCH`.

### Central icon

- Use a flat, clean, vector-like cultural symbol rather than a photographed product.
- The emblem must be recognizable at thumbnail size.
- Prefer one bold primary silhouette over a detailed arrangement of several objects.
- Keep details intentional and avoid photorealism, gemstones, pearls, or bracelet parts unless that material is itself the subject.
- Do not place a circle, scalloped medallion, badge, plate, halo, or layered frame behind the central icon.

### Meaning panel

- Use exactly three meanings unless the user approves a different structure.
- Keep each label to roughly one to three words.
- Pair each meaning with a simple, visually distinct vector icon.
- Meanings should connect directly to the featured element and remain culturally defensible.
- Avoid repeating the same icon for different ideas.

## Color system

Color is flexible, not template-locked. Build each card from these functional roles:

- **Paper:** warm ivory or a very light tint derived from the element.
- **Atmosphere:** one or two pale background pastels.
- **Primary element:** the most recognizable natural or traditional color of the featured symbol.
- **Supporting element:** one quiet complementary pastel.
- **Line and type:** a muted dark tone such as plum, aubergine, warm charcoal, or softened brown.
- **Metallic accent:** restrained champagne or antique gold; never highly reflective or dominant.

Avoid neon color, stark black, pure digital white, oversaturated red, and harsh gradients. White-led cards should still have subtle ivory or opalescent shading so they do not feel flat.

## Korean visual vocabulary

Use selectively, not all at once:

- Hanji paper texture
- Crescent moon and soft cloud forms
- Korean lattice-inspired corner geometry
- Restrained dancheong-inspired linework and color relationships
- Bojagi-inspired color blocking where relevant
- Floral or scalloped medallion frames
- Maedeup knot and tassel forms for pouch or accessory imagery

Do not use decoration merely because it appears “East Asian.” Every major motif should support the Korean identity or the featured element.

## Approved examples

### Maehwa / Plum Blossom

- **Palette:** moonlit lavender, pale periwinkle, ivory, muted aubergine, champagne gold.
- **Icon:** stylized five-petal plum blossoms and branch; no pearl or gemstone center.
- **Meanings:** Quiet strength · Perseverance · New beginnings.

### Pearl

- **Palette:** pearl white, warm ivory, opalescent blue-lilac, silver gray, champagne gold.
- **Icon:** clean pearl symbol; no bracelet photograph.
- **Meanings:** Purity · Wisdom · Calm and grace.

### Yeongeun / Lotus Root

- **Palette:** warm hanji ivory, creamy oat, pale celadon, soft sand beige, muted lotus pink, antique gold.
- **Icon:** one front-facing lotus-root slice with clearly readable natural holes and, at most, one small leaf accent.
- **Meanings:** Resilience · Connection · Growth.

### Bokjumeoni / Lucky Pouch

- **Palette:** a soft pastel reinterpretation of obangsaek/saekdong—warm coral red, muted indigo, pale jade, soft golden yellow, warm ivory, dusty plum, and antique gold—on a warm ivory-to-peach ground.
- **Icon:** one simple rounded Korean lucky-pouch silhouette with a gathered top, single drawstring, one maedeup-inspired knot, one short tassel, and no more than one restrained geometric motif.
- **Meanings:** Good fortune · Prosperity · Protection.

## Pre-publish checklist

- [ ] Primary title spelling is correct.
- [ ] English subtitle is present directly beneath the title.
- [ ] The central icon depicts only the intended element and sits directly on the background without a medallion or layered badge.
- [ ] No product photograph or unintended pearl/gemstone appears.
- [ ] Three meanings are concise, distinct, and culturally appropriate.
- [ ] Each meaning has a different, readable icon.
- [ ] Palette is pastel, soft, and derived from the featured element.
- [ ] Korean motifs feel intentional rather than generic.
- [ ] All text is legible at Etsy/mobile thumbnail size.
- [ ] Card remains visually consistent with the approved Dalbit family.

## Product to Card Matching Workflow

Use this workflow whenever a product listing includes a Dalbit cultural symbol card.

1. Confirm the product identity from the Etsy listing, product master, and current product images. Record the primary material, secondary material, motif, color family, and any verified cultural reference.
2. Match the card to the featured element, not merely to the bracelet color.
3. Use the card for the primary identifying material first, then add a secondary card only when it explains another prominent verified element.
4. Keep unrelated cultural cards out of the listing. Do not use a Maehwa card for a bracelet that has no verified plum blossom motif, and do not use a Bokjumeoni card unless the product is inspired by a Korean lucky pouch.
5. Keep the photo sequence consistent: hero image, video, signature packaging, size or material information, detail images, worn or in use image, then the matching meaning card or cards.
6. If only one meaning card can be used, choose the card for the product's strongest distinguishing material or motif.

## Etsy Product Title and Description Workflow

Use only verified product information. Read the current Etsy listing, relevant product records, the image set, the card match, and any approved keyword research before writing.

### Title

1. Lead with the product type.
2. Follow with the strongest distinguishing material or motif.
3. Add a useful color, cultural, styling, or gifting term when it is supported by the product.
4. Keep the title clear and readable within 140 characters. Avoid repetitive keyword strings.

### Description

1. Begin with a short, warm opening grounded in the product's visible design.
2. Name the product and describe its verified materials, colors, construction, fit, and distinctive details.
3. Look up wearable length or dimensions in the Product Master using the exact Variant SKU before writing measurements. If the SKU has minimum and maximum rows, present the result as an adjustable range.
4. Do not use packed shipping dimensions as wearable measurements.
5. Explain cultural inspiration only when it is verified. Separate cultural inspiration from manufacturing origin.
6. Add one concise sentence about wearing, using, layering, or gifting the product.
7. Add a `DETAILS` section with short bullets containing only applicable facts.
8. Include the signature packaging paragraph, adapting the product noun where necessary.
9. Include care instructions only when supplied or verified.
10. Avoid unsupported claims such as hypoallergenic, waterproof, sustainable, guaranteed good luck, or performance benefits.
11. Avoid a `Sold as` section and do not leave internal notes or placeholders in customer facing copy.
12. Outside headings and bullet lists, avoid unnecessary hyphens. Use plain phrasing such as `gold plated` and `Korean inspired` unless a hyphen is needed for clarity.

### Product Master Length Lookup

1. Identify the exact Etsy listing SKU.
2. Search the `Product Master` spreadsheet in the `Products` tab using the Variant SKU.
3. Read the `Variant / packaging` values for the matching SKU rows.
4. When separate minimum and maximum rows exist, convert them to a customer friendly range while preserving the source values.
5. Add the range to the Etsy `DETAILS` section only when it describes the wearable product. Keep shipping weight and packed dimensions separate.

Example: SKU `B07 Pink` has `B07 Pink (MIN)` at `6 3/8 inches` and `B07 Pink (MAX)` at `10 4/8 inches`, so the listing may state an adjustable length of approximately `6⅜ to 10½ inches`.

### Tags

- Provide exactly 13 comma separated Etsy tags.
- Keep every tag at 20 characters or fewer, including spaces.
- Cover the product type, materials, design details, cultural terms, styling or use, and gifting intent.
- Avoid duplicate, vague, misspelled, or unrelated tags.

### Final Review

Before publishing, compare the title, description, tags, image order, and meaning cards against the same verified product facts. The copy and cards should describe the same product without introducing a new material, motif, symbolism, or claim.

## Living-document update protocol

After each card is reviewed:

1. Record explicit user feedback as either a one-card exception or a reusable system rule.
2. Add reusable rules to the relevant section of this guide only after they are accepted in the final design.
3. Add the approved card to **Approved examples**, including title, subtitle, palette, icon, and meanings.
4. Update the checklist when a repeated rendering problem or quality issue is discovered.
5. Increment the version: patch for wording/examples, minor for a new reusable rule, major for a layout-system change.
6. Add a dated changelog entry so the guide improves without losing decision history.
7. Never silently replace a previously approved rule; preserve the change and reason in the changelog.

This document improves when it is updated during future Dalbit Studio work. It does not change autonomously outside an active editing session.
