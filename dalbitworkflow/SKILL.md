# Dalbit product workflow

Run one coordinated workflow using [dalbitmaster](../dalbitmaster/SKILL.md), [dalbitsize](../dalbitsize/SKILL.md), [dalbitcard](../dalbitcard/SKILL.md), and [dalbitlisting](../dalbitlisting/SKILL.md). Read each specialist skill when its stage begins and follow its current instructions for sources, verification, outputs, and limitations. The user's current instructions take precedence. Keep the complete SKU, including any variant suffix, as the identity key throughout; do not transfer facts or assets between similar products.

Read [dalbitbrand.md](<DALBIT_WORKSPACE>/dalbitbrand.md) before motif research, cultural copy, card design, or listing work so the shared Korean cultural and brand standards carry across stages.

## Start and resume

- If the user names a SKU or product, resolve it against the live Product Master and its local SKU folder. If no product is named, start with `dalbitmaster`'s scan of immediate SKU folders under `<DALBIT_ROOT>` and work through genuinely missing products. Group missing size and price questions as that skill requires. An existing but incomplete Master row is a separate issue; do not create a duplicate.
- After discovery, scope each specialist stage to the active SKU or approved small batch. Do not let a specialist's general root-folder instructions expand this run to unrelated products.
- Use the live Product Master as the record for SKU facts, chosen motif labels, cultural brief and sources, and product status. Use the current conversation for stage feedback and approvals, and verify existing size photos, cards, and Etsy assets at their live destinations. Do not create separate per-SKU Markdown progress files or duplicate the sheet as a tracker.
- On a follow-up or request to resume, recheck the live Master, files, Drive, Etsy, and the user's latest feedback. Continue from the first unapproved stage. If an earlier approval is unavailable in a later chat, ask for that stage's review after reconstructing its output from live sources. Never treat silence, elapsed time, or an unrelated reply as approval. Do not rerun a completed stage merely because the workflow resumed.

## Stages and review checkpoints

**Current b44–b48 batch exception (October 26, 2026):** The owner saved the new hero title styling for future use but excluded this batch. At stage 4, use the original thumbnail treatment without the new title/sentiment/dividers. The b44 title-treated preview is unused and is not approved for Etsy. Keep the usual thumbnail and listing review checkpoint.

1. **Product Master — `dalbitmaster`.** Find missing SKU folders, inspect their own photos, compare accurate wording in similar Master rows, collect each missing product's size and price from the user, then add and read back its row. For each bracelet, review its meaningful decorative piece or pieces, such as a focal charm, stone, bead, or pendant, without assigning meaning to incidental hardware or every bead. Describe the visible shape and details, suggest a motif name, and use the user's correction or chosen name as the final product label. Research each proposed Korean cultural connection in reliable museum or cultural sources, distinguishing documented symbolism from a modern brand interpretation. Write `Korean Cultural Element — [English name] ([Korean name])` and one concise, warm English sentence explaining the connection for each supported element. Flag an unclear motif, material, or species briefly; do not invent symbolism or claim a motif is uniquely Korean. Save the label, cultural brief, source, and any uncertainty in the matching Product Master row. For a SKU already present, verify its unique row without duplicating it. Show the row's key facts, motif brief, any uncertainties, and the verified sheet result. Ask for feedback before moving to the size stage. If the user corrects a field, update only the exact SKU row when authorized, verify the saved value, and show the corrected sheet result.
2. **Size photo — `dalbitsize`.** For an eligible bracelet, require a unique Master row with clear size and closure, then make or locate its finished three-line image. The `Material` line must name the main visible beads or cord **and the main decorative piece or pieces**, such as a focal charm or pendant, without guessing composition. Show the image and saved path for feedback. If the product is not an eligible bracelet, record why this stage was skipped and continue. If a required fact or suitable full-bracelet photo is missing, ask for that input and keep this SKU pending while other ready SKUs can progress.
3. **Cultural card — `dalbitcard`.** Use the Master’s verified `Korean design element/inspiration` and the SKU's cultural brief to find a suitable existing card or create the card, following its local and Drive checks. Show the selected or new card and its locations for feedback. If the element is blank or uncertain without a user-chosen label, ask the user to identify it or confirm that this stage should be skipped; never invent a motif or cultural meaning. Revise a card when the user requests changes, then show the revised version before advancing.
4. **Etsy listing — `dalbitlisting`.** Resolve the same SKU to one identified listing in `DalbitStudioStore`. Prepare copy, tags, a photo-faithful hero following [dalbitlisting's title-treatment reference](../dalbitlisting/references/hero-styling.md), and an ordered media plan using the accepted assets. Preserve the original SKU photo and background; add a Product Master-derived title and sentiment with thin top/bottom dividers inspired by the approved Dalbit cards, then finish framing toward about 40% product fill where the original allows. Do not create a new scene or add props. Before creating or updating an Etsy listing, changing listing copy, or uploading media, show the final title-treated hero inline with its SKU and saved path and obtain the user's explicit approval of that preview. For a batch, show every proposed hero and identify which SKUs are approved. Earlier stage approval or general listing authorization does not approve an unseen thumbnail. Show the exact proposed Etsy title, description, tags, media files, and any current-listing fields that would change, and obtain approval of those changes as well; both approvals may be collected in the same review packet. If the hero is revised, show it and obtain approval again. On approval, use the listing skill's fresh-read and verification procedure to update only approved copy and upload only approved media, using the exact approved hero file for rank 1. If no existing listing can be identified, use the listing skill's authenticated API draft-creation procedure after package approval. Check for duplicates, save the exact SKU and approved inventory, and verify the new draft and its media. Keep it unpublished unless the user explicitly authorizes publishing.

For listing-stage video uploads, inspect the matching SKU's footage and select the best continuous five-second segment yourself, prioritizing minimal camera shake, natural movement, sharp focus, and an effective clear view of the bracelet and its focal pieces. Follow `dalbitlisting`'s five-second video preparation rules: preserve the original, trim a separate `<SKU>_etsy_video_5s.mp4` at natural speed, inspect the whole finished clip, and include it with its source time range in the media review packet before uploading approved media. Do not automatically use the opening five seconds. Preserve an existing active Etsy video unless replacement is requested.

After each completed stage, provide one compact review packet for the affected SKU or small batch: what was produced, links or images to inspect, unresolved facts, and a direct request to approve or describe changes. Do not start the next substantive stage for that SKU until the user responds. If the user approves, continue automatically in the same turn; if they request changes, revise the current stage and present it again. A skipped stage needs a brief explanation but no approval unless skipping changes the intended product presentation.

## Boundaries

- Preserve each specialist skill's duplicate checks and source hierarchy. Before any write or upload, re-read the relevant live destination and confirm the SKU, listing, and existing assets still match. Keep generated originals and previously approved files unless the user requests replacement.
- Completing the Master, size, or card stage may write the outputs specified by those skills before its review checkpoint. The listing review must occur before Etsy copy changes or media uploads. Drafting listing copy may update the keyword database as `dalbitlisting` specifies; report that in the review packet.
- Do not claim the workflow is complete until the applicable stages are approved and their outputs verified. When a source or tool is unavailable, explain the specific gap and resume from the last live verified state after it is resolved.
