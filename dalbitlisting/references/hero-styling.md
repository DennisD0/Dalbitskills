# Dalbit thumbnail title treatment

Read this before making or revising a rank-1 hero thumbnail. On October 26, 2026, the owner supplied [this example](../assets/hero-style-reference.png), then clarified: borrow **only the title design and incorporate it into the existing thumbnail photo**, not a new product scene. The owner also requested that the lines above and below the title use design language similar to the Dalbit cultural cards.

## Title and sentiment

**Current batch exception (owner instruction, October 26, 2026):** Keep this new styling saved for future work, but do not apply it to b44–b48 in the current batch. Use their original thumbnail treatment without the new title, sentiment or decorative dividers. The saved `b44_etsy_hero_40pct.png` is an unused styling preview, not an approved listing asset. Do not select it automatically from the SKU folder. Existing thumbnail review requirements still apply.

- Use elegant readable serif lettering, an occasional restrained script accent, generous spacing, and a concise product title with one short warm sentence. Choose warm brown or a coordinated muted dark color that is legible against the actual photo.
- Derive the wording from the exact SKU's live Product Master product name, `Korean design element/inspiration`, supported cultural brief and owner-selected labels. Shorten the name without inventing a material or motif. Do not copy the example's good-luck sentence to unrelated products. Keep editorial wishes distinct from promises of luck, protection, health or other effects.
- Put the overlay in clear space away from all jewelry. Include the exact proposed title and sentence in the image prompt and review packet. Do not add SKU, price, measurements, discounts, unsupported services, or a new logo. Korean translation is optional only when requested or clearly useful, accurate and readable.

## Lines above and below the title

Inspect an approved Dalbit card, such as [Blue Stone](../../dalbitcard/assets/approved-cards/blue-stone.png) or [Pearl](../../dalbitcard/assets/approved-cards/pearl.png). Create two light, balanced horizontal ornaments framing the title/caption area. Echo the cards' restrained Korean lattice-inspired geometry, fine antique-gold or coordinated muted lines, and small soft crescent/cloud details where suitable. Keep the ornaments secondary to the product and title. Do not paste the whole card frame, header, side lettering, footer panel or central subject onto the photo, or default to generic heart/floral flourishes from the example.

## Preserve the original thumbnail photograph

- Start with the untouched original in `C:\Users\sshoo\Dalbit\<SKU>`, not a previous generated thumbnail. The example is a **typography/layout reference only**, never a product or background reference.
- Preserve the original background, fabric, lighting, shadows and colors. Do not replace or extend the background, add dried flowers or other props, invent a scene, or regenerate the jewelry. The satin and flowers in the reference are not part of the requested change.
- When the owner requires the local background to remain exactly unchanged, generate only a transparent lettering/divider layer and composite it over the original photograph. Keep the original pixels outside that overlay; use a faithful crop for framing. Do not send the whole photograph through a generative edit that could redraw the fabric or jewelry. If the owner identifies an existing local thumbnail as the target, use that exact file rather than substituting another photo.
- Preserve every bead and its arrangement, all charms, closures, chain tails, material appearance and proportions. If editing changes those details or the original background, correct it or use a more faithful approach before delivery.
- Apply this treatment only to the rank-1 hero. Size photos, packaging, details, worn photos, videos and symbol cards retain their own workflows.

## Framing and zoom

Plan the title, sentence and two dividers so that the final crop retains all text and jewelry. Apply the title treatment and then finish framing/zoom, or achieve both faithfully in one pass. Preserve the requested listing aspect ratio; the portrait reference does not set the aspect ratio. The current b44–b48 batch uses 1:1 and is excluded from this new styling as stated above.

The owner clarified that the bracelet should occupy **about 40% of the final frame**, leaving room for the title and sentiment. This is a product-fill target, not a 40% enlargement or the earlier 70% default. Include every charm, closure and tail. Do not force the percentage by rebuilding the background or changing the product. If the original framing cannot support it through a faithful crop, choose another original or report the framing limitation. Product fidelity, complete visibility and readable copy take priority.

## Save and review

- Check for a suitable existing output before generating another. Preserve originals and approved assets. Save the final inside the exact SKU folder as `<SKU>_etsy_hero_40pct.png`, using a versioned sibling on collision unless replacement was requested. If a separate title-overlay intermediate is needed, use `<SKU>_etsy_hero_title_overlay.png`.
- Compare against the original at full and thumbnail size: unchanged product/background, exact title and sentence, restrained card-style dividers, readable text, appropriate fill, no cropped component or lettering. Report a material framing limitation rather than claiming the target was met.
- Show every final hero inline with its SKU, absolute saved path, and exact overlay wording. Obtain approval of that final preview and the proposed listing copy/media plan before Etsy creation, edits or uploads. A revised thumbnail needs a new preview approval; Master, size and card approvals do not cover it.
